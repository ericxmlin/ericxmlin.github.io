About me

I’m an undergraduate at Carnegie Mellon University. I enjoy problems that start with a simple question and lead to a bigger one, like how to use patterns in data to compress a file, or how to tell whether a model is applying a rubric consistently.

I’m drawn to probability, machine learning, and the algorithms behind everyday software. Outside of coding, I also enjoy learning about astronomy, especially exoplanets and what makes a planet habitable.
